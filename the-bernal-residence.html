The Bernal Residence

Nearly every system in the house, on a fixed move-in date.

Overview

Nearly every system in the house, at once

The Bernal Residence was a comprehensive home renovation involving nearly every system of the house. The scope included the renovation of three bathrooms, a new kitchen, new flooring throughout, new impact windows, a new roof, pool deck tile replacement, pool resurfacing, full plumbing replacement including the removal of old cast iron pipes, air conditioning relocation, a new electrical panel, exterior stucco repairs, and full interior and exterior painting.

Why this project matters

The Bernal Residence reflects our commitment to integrity. Completing a complex renovation under a tight schedule while navigating contract alignment required calm leadership and clear decision-making. The project was completed in early 2025, with systems updated and the home ready for its next chapter.

Scope of work

  • Three bathrooms and a new kitchen
  • New roof and impact windows
  • Full plumbing replacement, cast iron removed
  • Pool resurfacing and deck tile
  • New electrical panel and stucco repairs

The challenge

A fixed move-in date across every trade

What we found

The schedule was tight from the start, with a fixed move-in date that required disciplined planning. As the project progressed, a discrepancy between the signed agreement and a revised contract version came to light late in the process. To protect clarity and fairness for all parties, construction was paused while the contract terms were reviewed and aligned back to the original agreement. Although this created temporary tension, addressing it directly allowed the project to move forward with transparency.

How we solved it

We sequenced the trades so none of them blocked the next, and put every adjustment in writing as it came up.

What we learned

What a whole-house renovation taught us

  1. 01ScopeSequence the whole houseContract clarity must be protected at every stage.
  2. 02DeadlineProtect the move-in dateScope changes should always be documented immediately.
  3. 03TradesOne trade at a timePausing work can be the responsible choice when alignment is lost.
  4. 04ReportingSay it in writingClear communication preserves long-term trust.
