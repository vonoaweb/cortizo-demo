RODRIGUEZ RESIDENCE

The Rodriguez Residence was a home renovation with key structural work. We demolished a detached studio, renovated three bathrooms, and opened the kitchen by removing a structural wall and installing a steel beam. New impact windows and sliding doors increased light and improved the indoor-outdoor connection.

SAN JUAN HOTEL

The San Juan Hotel project meant stepping into an open, unfinished 2018 permit and bringing the cafeteria kitchen back on track. We assessed existing work, coordinated inspections, and delivered a compliant, fully renewed system with a new grease trap, lift station and plumbing.

See project

THE BERNAL RESIDENCE

The Bernal Residence was a comprehensive renovation that rethought how the home looks, feels and performs. Our scope included three new bathrooms, a new kitchen, new flooring throughout, impact windows, a new roof, and a refreshed pool area with new deck tile, resurfacing and complete plumbing replacement.
