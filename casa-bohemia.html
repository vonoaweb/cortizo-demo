Casa Bohemia

Our first full build, and the project that defined our standards.

Overview

Our first build, from the foundation up

Casa Bohemia was Cortizo Construction’s first full new construction project. Built entirely from the ground up, this home marked a turning point for our company and became the foundation for the systems, standards and process we follow today. We were responsible for the structural work and all plumbing installations, coordinating each phase from foundation through completion.

Why this project matters

Casa Bohemia shaped how we work today. From this project we established the non-negotiable standards that guide every job we take on.

Scope of work

  • Structural work, foundation to completion
  • All plumbing installations
  • Phase coordination across trades
  • Permitting and inspections

The challenge

Every decision had to be made before the first wall

What we found

New construction requires decisions long before the first wall is built. Every structural element and plumbing line had to be planned, with little room for improvisation. This project demanded precision, sequencing, and a deep understanding of how systems interact.

How we solved it

We planned every structural element and plumbing line before the first wall went up, and coordinated the trades from the start instead of solving it on the way.

What we learned

What the first ground-up build taught us

  1. 01DrawingsStructure firstStructure sets the tone for everything that follows.
  2. 02TradesTrades togetherEarly coordination between trades prevents delays and rework.
  3. 03SystemsAligned from day onePlumbing and structural decisions must be aligned from the start.
  4. 04BuildPlanning over urgencyClear planning reduces uncertainty during construction.
