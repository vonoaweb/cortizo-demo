Rodriguez Residence

A full renovation that lived or died on sequencing.

Overview

A renovation that lived or died on sequencing

The Rodriguez Residence was a comprehensive home renovation that required careful planning, structural coordination and strong schedule management. The scope included the complete demolition of a detached studio, the renovation of three bathrooms including the relocation of one, and a full kitchen transformation. To create an open and functional kitchen we removed a structural wall and installed a metal beam with supporting columns, which also required partial roof reconstruction. The project included new impact windows and new sliding doors, improving the home’s connection between interior and exterior spaces.

Why this project matters

The Rodriguez Residence confirmed that a successful renovation is not defined by how fast it moves, but by how well it adapts. By allowing flexibility in the schedule we finished before the client’s move-in date, turning a potential problem into a positive experience.

Scope of work

  • Detached studio demolition
  • Three bathrooms, one relocated
  • Structural wall removal and steel beam
  • Partial roof reconstruction
  • Impact windows and sliding doors

The challenge

When the cabinets arrive late and the family is moving in

What we found

This project tested scheduling more than anything else. With multiple trades, inspections, custom materials and structural modifications involved, sequencing became critical. A delay in the kitchen cabinets caused by a paint issue required us to adjust the construction flow while protecting the client’s move-in timeline. Rather than rushing the final stages, we reorganized the schedule to maintain quality and reduce stress for the homeowners.

How we solved it

Rather than rushing the final stages, we reorganised the schedule to hold the quality and protect the family’s move-in date.
