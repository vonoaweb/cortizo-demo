Laura Cortizo

Laura Cortizo is the driving force behind Cortizo Construction, a Miami-based general contractor devoted to the art of construction through the lens of hospitality.

Born in Cienfuegos, Cuba, Laura’s connection to construction began early in life. She grew up watching her family build homes and create beautifully crafted furniture through woodworking, experiences that quietly shaped her understanding of making, care and intention. At twelve, Laura and her family migrated to Spain, where diverse landscapes, historic cities and architectural traditions deepened her curiosity for the built environment.

Nearly a decade rooted in Miami, Laura’s relationship with construction has become more than a profession. It is a connection to people, to process, and to the quiet devotion of showing up day after day. She holds profound admiration for the men and women in construction, for their commitment, resilience and capacity to build through presence, repetition and care.

For Laura, construction is inseparable from the human experience. It is about hands, time, discipline and trust. Every project represents respect for the people who build it and the lives that will inhabit it.

This path is shaped by lineage, movement and meaning. Join the journey. Let’s build.
