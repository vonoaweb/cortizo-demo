What we do

Two kinds of work, one way of working

Most of what we build is residential: full home renovations with structural work, and houses built from the ground up. The rest is hospitality, where the job is usually to bring a kitchen or a space back into service without closing the business down around it.

Both run the same way. Complete plans before anyone starts, trades coordinated early, schedules with room in them, and everything that changes put in writing.

Laura Cortizo

Founder & Director

Clear guidance. Honest communication. Elevated craftsmanship. A construction experience designed to feel effortless.

Laura Cortizo is the driving force behind Cortizo Construction, a Miami-based general contractor devoted to the art of construction through the lens of hospitality.

Born in Cienfuegos, Cuba, Laura’s connection to construction began early in life. She grew up watching her family build homes and create beautifully crafted furniture through woodworking, experiences that quietly shaped her understanding of making, care, and intention.

At twelve, Laura and her family migrated to Spain. Her world expanded through diverse landscapes, historic cities, and architectural traditions. Those years deepened her curiosity and appreciation for the built environment, planting a lasting passion for the art of building.

Nearly a decade rooted in Miami, a city crucial to her personal and professional growth, Laura’s relationship with construction has become more than a profession. It is a deep connection to people, to process, and to the quiet devotion of showing up day after day. She holds profound admiration for the men and women in construction, for their commitment, resilience, and capacity to build through presence, repetition, and care.

For Laura, construction is inseparable from the human experience. It is about hands, time, discipline, and trust. Every project represents respect for the people who build it and the lives that will inhabit it.

At the core of Laura’s perspective is the belief that construction is not only about structures, but about experiences and people. Spaces carry emotion, memory, and intention. Building them thoughtfully is both a craft and a form of care.

This path is shaped by lineage, movement, and meaning.
